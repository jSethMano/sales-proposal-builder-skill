# Case studies

## CS-001 | Regional freight company moves 85 staff to Microsoft 365
Industry: Logistics
Summary: A regional freight company was running on an ageing on-premises mail server. We planned the move with their operations team, migrated mailboxes and shared files over two weekends, and kept dispatch running throughout.
Outcome: All 85 staff were working in Microsoft 365 on the Monday after cutover, with no lost email.

## CS-002 | Accounting practice gets round-the-clock support in tax season
Industry: Professional services
Summary: A 40-person accounting practice needed help outside business hours during tax season. We moved them to Managed IT Support (Plus) and added Microsoft 365 Backup.
Outcome: Priority 1 incidents during their busiest quarter were answered within the 30-minute target.

## CS-003 | Three-site retailer standardizes its networks
Industry: Retail
Summary: A retailer with three stores had a different firewall and wireless setup at each site. We replaced and standardized the equipment, then took over day-to-day network management.
Outcome: One configuration across all three stores, with monthly reports on uptime and changes.
