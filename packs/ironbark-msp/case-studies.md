# Case studies

## CS-001 | Dental group gets its four clinics on one setup
Industry: Healthcare
Summary: A dental group with four clinics had a different IT setup in each one. We brought every clinic onto Helpdesk and Support and Device Management, and set up every computer the same way.
Outcome: One setup across four clinics, and new staff now get a working laptop on their first day.

## CS-002 | Engineering firm locks down its accounts
Industry: Engineering
Summary: A 30-person engineering firm was worried about email scams after a near miss. We ran a Cyber Security Uplift, turned on multi-factor sign-in for everyone and tightened their email settings.
Outcome: Every account is now protected by multi-factor sign-in, and the firm passed its insurer's security questionnaire.

## CS-003 | Not-for-profit moves off old servers
Industry: Not-for-profit
Summary: A not-for-profit with 45 staff and volunteers was running email and files on two ageing servers. We moved everything to Microsoft 365 over one weekend and added Cloud Backup.
Outcome: The old servers were switched off, and staff could work from any office or from home.
